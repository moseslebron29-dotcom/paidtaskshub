Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

What we collect

Your full name, username, email address, phone number, a securely hashed password, your task activity, wallet records, and M-Pesa payment references (amount, phone, receipt number, date).

What we never collect

We never see or store your M-Pesa PIN. You enter it only on your own phone.

How we use it

To run your account, verify your email, process your activation payment and withdrawals, prevent fraud and contact you about your account.

Sharing

We share only what is needed with Safaricom (M-Pesa payments) and our email provider (verification codes). We do not sell your data.

Security

Passwords are hashed, sessions use secure cookies, and payment credentials stay on our server.

Your choices

You can update your profile in your account, or contact support to ask for your data or account deletion.
