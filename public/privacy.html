Privacy Notice

PaidTasksHub collects account details such as username, email address and phone number to operate member accounts, send verification messages, process activation payments and manage task submissions and withdrawals.

Email and payment services

Email verification messages are sent using Brevo. M-Pesa activation payments are processed through Safaricom Daraja. The platform stores payment references and payment status to reconcile transactions. Your payment credentials and M-Pesa PIN are never requested or stored by PaidTasksHub.

Account security

Passwords are stored as salted hashes. Session cookies are configured as HTTP-only and secure when served over HTTPS. Never share your password or one-time email verification code.

Data retention and requests

Account, task, payment and ledger records are retained as needed to operate the service, prevent fraud and resolve payment disputes. For account or privacy requests, contact the platform administrator.
