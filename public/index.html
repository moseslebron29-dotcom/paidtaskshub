GETTING STARTED

How PaidTasksHub works

Simple steps to get started and track your work.

01

Create an account

Register with your username, email, phone number and password. Verify your email using Brevo.

02

Activate your account

Pay the one-time KES 100 activation fee using the M-Pesa STK Push. Your account unlocks after Daraja confirms payment.

03

Complete & submit

Choose an available task, follow its instructions and submit proof. Approved task rewards are added to your wallet.

Task rewards depend on available partner work and successful review. No income is guaranteed. Never pay anyone outside the official activation checkout.
