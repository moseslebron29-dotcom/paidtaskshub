Frequently asked questions

What do I need to join?

A smartphone with internet, an email address you can open, and an M-Pesa registered phone number.

Do I have to pay to activate my account?

No. Registration is free and there is no activation fee. Be careful of anyone who asks you to send money to unlock earnings on PaidTasksHub.

How much can I earn?

Rewards depend on the tasks available and are shown on each task before you begin. Earnings are not guaranteed, and some tasks may have daily limits.

How do withdrawals work?

You request a withdrawal from your wallet (minimum applies). Requests are reviewed, then paid to your registered M-Pesa number. Your history shows each step, and a withdrawal is marked paid only after M-Pesa confirms it.

Why was my account suspended?

Accounts can be suspended for fraud, duplicate accounts or abuse of tasks. Contact support below if you think this was a mistake.
